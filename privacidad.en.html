Privacy policy

Mil Vidas. Effective November 5, 2026.

What data we collect

We don't collect any. Mil Vidas requires no sign-up, has no ads, uses no analytics, and doesn't send your information to any server of ours.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We don't see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases you own and saves that list on your device to activate them. Google handles that data under its own policy.

Where your game is saved

Your game, achievements, and settings are saved only on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are lost. You can make a backup from Settings, and that copy stays in your hands.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Third parties

Mil Vidas doesn't share data with anyone, because it doesn't collect any. Google Play may collect installation data under its own policies.

Minors

The game is fiction for people 18 and older: it includes crime, violence, alcohol, and simulated gambling with no real money. It is not directed at minors.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version here. For any questions, use the contact email on the app's Google Play listing.
